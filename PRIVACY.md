# Privacy Policy

calendario-uni is a personal tool used only by its author.

- It reads the author's own Moodle calendar feed.
- It creates and updates events in the author's own Google Calendar,
  using the Google Calendar API.
- All data is processed locally on the author's computer.
- No data is collected, stored on external servers, sold, or shared
  with third parties.
- Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account
  permissions page.
